A visual-first front page summing up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026, drawn in a hand-drawn style with wobbly double-struck outlines on textured paper. One big picture leads: Sanofi's $41 billion cash purchase of Insmed, drawn as a giant Brinsupri inhaler under a gold $41B burst. Below it, four more stories with drawn icons: the Israeli strike that killed Hamas internal security chief Naeem Abu Nahel in Gaza City; fourteen nations jointly recognising the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, with the UK sanctioning five generals; the UN Secretary-General's call for a multilateral AI risk framework within a year; and COP31 in Antalya ending without agreement, the first failed COP since 2009. An ALSO TODAY strip adds the reported Russian nuclear-armed satellite plan and South Korea scrambling jets over its air-defence zone. Every story is drawn at build time from reporting corroborated by at least two outlets; the program copies the finished image to the output directory.
